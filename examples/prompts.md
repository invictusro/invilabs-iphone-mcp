# Example prompts

Use your phone's code from the panel in place of `SE001`.

## Getting started

- "List my Invi Labs phones and tell me which ones are online."
- "Check whether SE001 is ready, then take a screenshot and describe the screen."

## Everyday tasks

- "On SE001, open Settings → General → About and tell me the iOS version and storage left."
- "Open Safari on SE001, search for the weather in Paris and read me the forecast."
- "On SE001, open the App Store page for Notes and tell me its rating. Don't install anything."

## Testing an app

- "Open my app on SE001, go through the sign-up screens without submitting anything, and list every screen with its text."
- "On SE001, open my app, tap each tab in the bottom bar and take a screenshot of each one. Tell me if any screen looks broken."

## Jailbroken phones (Invi Link)

- "List the apps on JB001 with their bundle IDs."
- "On JB001, dump the UI of the app on screen and tap the button labelled Continue."
- "On JB001, list the containers for Instagram and tell me which one is active."

## Read-only sessions

Connect with `https://api.invilabs.io/mcp?read_only=1` when the agent should only look: it gets screenshots, health and status tools, and nothing that changes the phone.
